BuildCraft - Minecraft 1.21.1 / NeoForge adaptation

Port author and maintainer: saltmoth
Original BuildCraft: SpaceToad, the BuildCraft team and all contributors

Thank you for the original pipes, engines, builders and quarry, and for the
work that made BuildCraft such a memorable part of Minecraft's industrial
history. The original metadata credits SpaceToad as creator and names SpaceToad
and the BuildCraft Team as authors. Original file-level copyright notices are
retained throughout the source.

Purpose and provenance
This independent, noncommercial adaptation aims to preserve the original
BuildCraft 7.99.24.7 experience as faithfully as possible on Minecraft 1.21.1.
It was made for the joy of playing the mod again, not for profit. saltmoth
maintains the platform adaptations and integration with IC2. That credit does
not replace the authorship of the original mod or its contributors.
